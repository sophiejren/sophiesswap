Cognitive Recipe N°01

"A Slow-Cooked Mind, in Five Ingredients"
serves 1 unsettled mind · prep time: a lifetime · best eaten while pacing
Sicilian Art
1 generous handful
for the colour, the baroque tilt, the beauty that doesn't apologise for being too much.
Systems Thinking
2 careful cups
scaffolding. feedback loops. second-order effects. measure precisely or the dish becomes a diagram.
Agent Workflows
a small swarm
small, autonomous, arguing among themselves. trust them to disagree productively.
Emotional Residue
a stubborn pinch
whatever lingered from yesterday, scraped from the bottom of the pan. this is the part that makes the dish yours.
Fusion Food
the medium itself
the medium, not an ingredient. nothing pure stays pure here. mediterranean + sichuan + cantonese, simmering.

"stir clockwise, until the meaning thickens." — the swamp cook

Method

  1. Begin with the residue. Always. Warm it gently — never boil it off. It's the only thing in here that can't be replaced.
  2. Add the Sicilian Art while the heat is still rising. Let it bloom and stain the broth a deep, slightly indecent gold.
  3. Stir in the systems thinking. Slowly, in concentric rings. Watch the patterns form. If you see a feedback loop, name it; if it sees you, leave the kitchen.
  4. Release the agents into the pot. They will swarm, sort, argue, and self-organise. Step back. Trust the swarm.
  5. Reduce until the meaning thickens. The broth should coat both a spoon and a thought.
  6. Plate generously into a wide, shallow bowl. The dish is never done — it just stops being hot.
