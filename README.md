### Hi, I'm Sophie 👋

I'm obsessed with **structure, cognition, and the human brain** — three scales of the same question: *how does intelligence organize itself?*

That throughline has shaped everything. I've architected systems at every scale — from **KFC, Hermès, Haier Group, and Bayer Pharmaceuticals** down to hundreds of small clinics across the US I work with today. Learning-science research at Stanford on how memory, curiosity, and play actually work. And now: building companies that sit at the intersection.

My corner of the internet lives at **[sophieren.com](https://sophieren.com)**.

#### How I work

I treat **architecture and taste as the same skill.** The same instinct that maps a million-line enterprise codebase to clean bounded contexts also maps the LOOMUS Library to seven categories that *mean* something. Same muscle.

Most of what I ship today, I ship in **collaboration with AI**. Pair-programming with Claude is my default workflow now — and it suits architects unusually well: describe the system, let the AI fill in the lines, review, iterate, refine taste.

I think this is the most interesting working mode of the next decade, and I'm trying to be early to it in a public way.
